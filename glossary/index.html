Glossary

Biology and modeling terms

Terms used in the biology guide, prediction task, and tutorials.

14 terms

Cytokine

A signaling protein that binds to cell receptors and can affect survival, division, and function. Its effects depend on the cell's receptors, state, and other signals.

Cytokine signaling →

CD4 T cell

A T cell with the CD4 protein on its surface. These include helper cells that coordinate immune responses and regulatory cells that limit them.

Cell identity and state →

Perturbation

A deliberate change used to measure a biological response. Here, cells receive individual cytokines or combinations, and their expression is compared with controls.

Condition

The experimental setting, including cytokines, doses, exposure duration, and activation protocol. Cytokine names alone do not define a condition.

Context

Biological and experimental factors that influence a response, including cell type, starting state, donor, dose, and measurement time.

Experimental context →

Gene-expression profile

A set of values, one per gene, for a cell or population. Single-cell RNA sequencing estimates RNA abundance from detected molecules; it does not directly measure protein abundance or cell function.

Single-cell measurements →

Latent representation

Features learned by a model to represent inputs such as expression profiles. A compact representation can capture patterns across many genes, including biological differences and technical effects.

Conditional generation

Generating observations for specified inputs. Here, a model generates expression profiles for a cytokine combination and cellular context, for comparison with measured cells.

Additive baseline

A reference model that adds individual effects. For A + B, it predicts the control mean plus the changes after A and B. Predictions depend on the control and expression scale.

Fit an additive baseline →

Non-additivity

A combined effect that differs from the sum of individual effects. Its interpretation depends on the gene, expression scale, dose, time, and control used for the comparison.

Held-out combination

A cytokine combination whose cells are all excluded from training. Combinations used for final testing must also be excluded from model selection and tuning.

Generalization tasks →

Highly variable genes

Genes selected for high variation across cells, often accounting for average expression. This reduces input size but may omit relevant genes. For model evaluation, select them using training data only.

Distributional prediction

Predicting variation across cells and the proportions of cell states. Populations with the same mean expression can have different distributions.

Model evaluation →

Virtual screen

A computational comparison of candidate perturbations, ranked by a defined response criterion. Experiments on selected conditions test the predictions.
