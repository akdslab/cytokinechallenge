Biological rationale

Cytokine interactions and combination prediction

Starting from naïve CD4 T cells gives comparisons across cytokine conditions a defined starting population. This matters because single-cell RNA sequencing consumes the sampled cells: responses are compared across populations, rather than in the same cells before and after treatment.

Individual cytokines establish reference effects; sampled combinations reveal departures from additivity. As the number of possible combinations grows, models must learn to predict conditions withheld from training. Our perspective on virtual cells explains the importance of cellular context.
