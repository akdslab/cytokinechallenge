Study design

Connect each prediction to an experimental condition

Match predictions to the cytokines, doses, exposure time, and starting cell population used in the experiment. Record the model version, training coverage, and candidate-selection rule before examining the measurements.

Use the detailed protocol for platform-specific procedures and experimental parameters. This overview explains how to organize the validation.

Experimental context

Controls, replication, and sample identity

Controls define the reference expression state. Individual-cytokine conditions allow the combined response to be compared with its component effects in the same context.

Document biological and technical replication and the distribution of donors and batches across conditions. Multiple cells from one sample do not replace independent biological replicates.

Preserve sample identifiers through quality control and analysis, with the criteria used to retain cells and genes.

Information accompanying each measurement

  • Cell source and starting state.
  • Cytokines, concentrations, and exposure times.
  • Control, donor, replicate, and batch assignments.
  • Measurement and quality-control procedures.
  • Gene identifiers and expression scale.
Validation analysis

Compare predicted and observed populations

Use a common gene set and expression scale. Compare changes relative to controls, variation within conditions, and the screen's predefined ranking. Report results for each condition.

Control and treated samples contain different cells, so compare their populations and distributions. Expression measurements test expression predictions; claims about cell function require a corresponding functional assay.

Keep measured and generated profiles labeled, with the model, data, protocol, and analysis-code versions.
