Prediction task

From cytokine conditions
to gene expression

Inputs: cytokine identities and available context, such as dose, exposure time, donor, and control profiles.

Outputs: predicted mean expression or a distribution of single-cell profiles for each condition.

Comparison: measured responses in held-out conditions. Control and treated samples contain different cells; compare populations rather than paired cell trajectories.

Suggested splits

Choose a generalization task

Start with held-out combinations in donors represented during training. Evaluate larger combinations and unseen donors separately.

A + B

New combinations of familiar cytokines

Keep each constituent cytokine and the evaluation donors represented in training. Hold out complete combinations to measure prediction beyond the observed conditions.

Recommended starting task

Illustrative designs, not release split files. A, B, and C denote cytokines at fixed doses and exposure times. Choose tasks supported by the measured conditions and donor coverage. CD8 T cells, B cells, and monocytes are coming soon.

Record the split assignments

Save train, validation, and test membership with stable sample and condition identifiers. Keep replicates of a held-out condition together. Fit preprocessing and select features on training data, tune on validation data, and reserve test responses for evaluation.

Comparable results

Evaluate the response your model predicts

Use the same split, available inputs, gene set, and expression scale for every model.

01 / Reference models

Control and additive baselines

Compare with no change from control and the sum of individual cytokine effects. Use baselines that have access to the same information as your model.

02 / Mean response

Changes in gene expression

Report prediction errors and changes relative to matched controls by condition. Correlation across all genes can conceal errors in responsive genes.

03 / Single-cell profiles

Variation between cells

For distributional predictions, also compare expression distributions and cell-state proportions. An accurate mean can miss changes in a subset of cells.

Report results by donor and number of cytokines combined, alongside training coverage. Distinguish scores over all modeled genes from scores over selected subsets. Base uncertainty estimates on biological replication, not cell count alone.
