Modeling approach

Learning responses to combinations

Learn from individual cytokines and sampled combinations to predict responses to unmeasured combinations.

The model encodes cells and cytokine effects as compact numerical vectors, called latent representations. It combines cytokine representations and maps the result to gene expression.

The nonlinear mapping allows a combination’s predicted response to differ from a sum of individual effects. Held-out combinations test whether this learned composition improves expression prediction.

Conditional generation

The model generates profiles for a specified cytokine combination and experimental context. Sampling multiple profiles gives a predicted distribution of responses within that condition.
